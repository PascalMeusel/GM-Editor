Suggested credit: **GM Editor project artwork, Pascal Meusel and contributors,
CC BY 4.0. Map and portrait artwork generated with OpenAI ImageGen and prepared
for the application; session-book icon drawn as original vector artwork.**

This grant applies only to rights held by the licensor and does not assert that
AI-generated pixels necessarily have copyright protection. It does not restrict
material already in the public domain or grant third-party trademark, privacy or
other rights. Fonts and audio retain their separately documented licenses.

The release inventory identifies exact file hashes and supporting records.
Unlisted private artwork, old icon files and omitted portrait species are not
part of this grant or the community preview. The first preview supplies two
collections of eleven SRD species, with eight portraits per sheet (176 portraits).
Legacy species names and stable IDs in source code exist for saved-data
compatibility; they are not additional bundled rules or a claim to third-party art.

The [content review](docs/open-source/CONTENT_REVIEW.md) records the selection,
AI provenance, attribution and exclusions.
