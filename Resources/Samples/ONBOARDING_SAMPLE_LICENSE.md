# Onboarding Sample Content

`Onboarding Sample - The Lantern Fair` is project-original tutorial content created for this repository.

It is intended as safe sample data for local testing, onboarding, screenshots, and demos. The names, scenario text, items, spells, NPCs, encounters, story notes, generated portraits, generated maps, generated audio cues, and handout text in the sample are fictional and are not copied from a published adventure.

When the historical sample is generated with `tools/seed_onboarding_sample.cpp`, its local package files live under `Resources/Campaigns/campaign_onboarding_lantern_fair/`. The `project.json` manifest and `Layouts/editor-prep.ini` file are project metadata for loading and presenting the sample; they do not introduce third-party adventure content.
